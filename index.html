FAQ

Does it work on Folia?

Yes, native region schedulers with automatic fallback.

Does it work on Spigot?

No. It needs Paper or a Paper fork such as Purpur, because it uses Paper's chat and text APIs.

Do I need Vault?

No, payouts fall back to console commands you configure.

Will it spam chat?

No. Broadcasts gate on player count, TPS, and cooldowns, and players can mute games.

Do I need a database?

No. Win tracking is on for new installs and can be turned off; servers upgrading from 1.4 keep their setting. Stats and leaderboards need it on.

Where is the full documentation?

Open the docs page for commands, permissions, and every config key.

Where do I get support?

Through the BuiltByBit resource page. Send your server version and the enable line.
